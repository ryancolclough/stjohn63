Privacy Policy

This page describes the public website of St. John's Lodge No. 63, A.F. & A.M., Carleton Place, Ontario.

Contact form

The contact form is handled by Formspree. If you send a message, the name, email address, phone number if you give one, inquiry type, and message are submitted through Formspree so the lodge can read them and reply. Membership questions can be sent that way.

Reaching the lodge

Write to secretary@stjohns63.ca. The lodge address and telephone number are on the contact page.

Fonts

Pages load the Cinzel and Inter typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files from Google. This site does not control how Google handles that request.

Tracking

This site does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking tools.

Messages

Messages are used to respond to you. The lodge does not sell personal information.

Other sites

Some pages link to the Grand Lodge of A.F. & A.M. of Canada in the Province of Ontario, a map of the lodge address, and historical documents hosted elsewhere. Those sites have their own practices.

See also the Accessibility Statement and Terms of Use.
