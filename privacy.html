Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy is for the public website of St. John's Lodge No. 63, A.F. & A.M., Carleton Place, Ontario, a lodge under the Grand Lodge of A.F. & A.M. of Canada in the Province of Ontario. It says what personal information the site collects, why, and who to write to. For this website, that person is the Lodge Secretary, at secretary@stjohns63.ca. The lodge address and telephone number are on the contact page.

Contact form

The contact form asks for your name, email address, telephone number if you wish to give one, the type of enquiry, and your message. Membership questions can be sent that way. The form is delivered by Formspree so the lodge can read it and reply. Sending the form is your consent for the lodge and Formspree to use those details for that purpose. You do not need an account to use the public pages.

Why it is collected

Messages are used to answer you. The lodge does not sell personal information, and it does not use it for advertising. The site collects only what the form asks for, and the photographs described below.

Who else handles it

Formspree receives the form so the message can reach the lodge. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The public pages do not contain the text of contact messages. Pages load the Cinzel and Inter typefaces from Google Fonts, and your browser requests those files from Google. The map link on the contact page opens Google Maps. This site does not control how those services handle a request, and it does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking tools.

How long it is kept

A contact message is kept long enough to reply and to keep a record of the enquiry. The lodge has not set a fixed number of months for that record. You may ask for a message to be deleted sooner. A signed photograph consent form is kept by the Lodge Secretary while the photograph or details remain on the site, and as a record that consent was given.

Access, correction, and removal

Write to secretary@stjohns63.ca to see the personal information this website holds about you, to correct it, or to have it removed. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Grand Lodge keeps its own records and publishes its own privacy policy at ontariomasons.ca/privacy-policy.

Photographs and Member Consent

A photograph of an identifiable lodge member, including an officer portrait, is published only with that member's written consent. The Lodge Secretary keeps the signed consent form on file.

Consent covers the photograph, the member's name, and the office held. Contact details are agreed separately.

A member may withdraw consent at any time by writing to secretary@stjohns63.ca. The photograph or those details will be removed promptly.

Group and event photographs of identifiable members follow the same rule.

Photographs on this site do not show Masonic ritual.

Other sites

Some pages link to other websites, including the Grand Lodge of A.F. & A.M. of Canada in the Province of Ontario and a map of the lodge address. Those sites are independent and have their own practices. A link is not an endorsement. The lodge reviews outward links from time to time, including at least every ninety days.

See also the Accessibility Statement and Terms of Use.
