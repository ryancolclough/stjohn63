Terms of Use

These terms apply to the public website of St. John's Lodge No. 63, A.F. & A.M., Carleton Place, Ontario, a lodge under the Grand Lodge of A.F. & A.M. of Canada in the Province of Ontario.

Using this site

The pages share general information about the lodge, its history, and how to get in touch. You may read them for personal, non-commercial purposes. Please use the site and the contact form lawfully and respectfully.

No advertising

This website does not carry advertising. Please do not use it to promote a product or service, and do not send commercial messages through the contact form.

Appropriate use

Do not attempt to disrupt the site, misuse the contact form, or present the lodge or Grand Lodge as endorsing a product, service, or outside organization.

Information on the site

The lodge tries to keep public information current. Dates for events can change. The members area is not open yet.

Reporting a problem

If something on this site is wrong, inaccessible, or being misused, write to secretary@stjohns63.ca. You may also notify the Grand Lodge webmaster at webmaster@grandlodge.on.ca.

See also the Privacy Policy and Accessibility Statement.
